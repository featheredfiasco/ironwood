Our Services

What We Fix, Install, and Maintain

Window repair, TV mounting, small home repairs, shower doors, exterior upkeep, and vacation rental turnover work — across the Emerald Coast.

Window Repair

Broken, foggy, or stuck windows are more than a nuisance — they let in moisture, air, and pests. We handle the full range of window repairs so your home or rental stays sealed, secure, and comfortable.

  • Broken glass replacement
  • Window screen repair & replacement
  • Sticking and stuck windows
  • Caulking around frames
  • Weatherstripping

TV Mounting & Small Installs

Clean mounts, level hangs, hidden cords where possible. We handle the installs that look simple until you're the one doing them — and we leave the wall, the fixture, and the room better than we found them.

  • TV wall mounting
  • Floating shelves
  • Curtain rods, blinds, and shades
  • Picture hanging and gallery walls
  • Mirror installation

Small Home Repairs

The everyday list — the stuff that's been on the fridge for three months. Send us the photos, we'll give you a quote, and we'll work through the list on one visit when we can.

  • Drywall patching and touch-up paint
  • Door and lock repair
  • Leaky faucets and running toilets
  • Loose handrails and cabinet hardware
  • Trim, baseboards, and quarter round

Shower Doors & Caulking

Old caulk and worn seals are how water damage starts. We re-caulk, re-seal, and install shower doors so your bathrooms stay dry, clean, and rental-ready.

  • Shower door installation
  • Re-caulking tubs and showers
  • Grout cleaning and touch-up
  • Sealing sinks, backsplashes, and countertops
  • Replacing worn or moldy seals

Exterior Upkeep

Salt air and humidity are hard on Gulf Coast homes. We handle the outside work that keeps a property looking cared for and holding its value.

  • Pressure washing (driveways, siding, decks)
  • Fence and gate repair
  • Siding and soffit touch-ups
  • Deck boards, railings, and stairs
  • Outdoor light fixture replacement

Vacation Rental & Real Estate Repairs

Turnover windows are tight, guest reviews are public, and listings don't wait. We work on STR schedules — fast, clean, and with clear communication so you know what's done and what's next.

  • Turnover punch lists between guests
  • Post-guest damage repairs
  • Pre-listing repairs for Realtors
  • Quick fixes between bookings
  • Move-in / move-out repairs
