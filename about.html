About IronWood

A local repair team for homes and vacation rentals.

Hi, we're Ples and Kelcie — the engaged couple behind IronWood Property Repair. We've been together since 2020, and we're building our business here in Fort Walton Beach. We work with homeowners and vacation rental managers across the Emerald Coast, and when you reach out about a repair, you'll hear directly from one of us.

Our Story

How IronWood came together.

Ples spent more than four years working in vacation rentals, where he saw firsthand how much it helps to have someone you can call when a repair comes up between guests. He also brings more than three years in glass repair and hands-on experience with windows. That background is why windows, glass shower doors, and rental repairs are at the center of what we do.

Kelcie brings more than a decade of marketing experience and works alongside Ples on repairs. She also helps with quotes, scheduling, and customer updates. We're both involved from the first conversation through the finished repair.

Meet the Owners

Ples and Kelcie.

We both bring different things to the business — Ples the hands-on repair experience, Kelcie the marketing and customer side — but we work on jobs together and we both answer when you reach out.

We think working with a small team should feel personal. When you call or message IronWood, you're talking directly with one of us.
